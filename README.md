**Churn Analysis Bank Customer Data Dashboard**
Recommended Structure and Order

1. Project Title / Headline: 📊 Churn Analysis Bank Customer Data Dashboard An interactive dashboard designed to analyze bank customer data, identify churn patterns, and provide actionable insights based on key demographic and behavioral features.

2. Short Description / Purpose: The Bank Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard offers a comprehensive view of customer retention and attrition factors. By visualizing customer profiles and churn rates across different attributes, it helps financial institutions understand where churn is most prevalent and which customer segments require attention for retention strategies.

3. Tech Stack: The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies: 💻 Power BI Desktop – Main platform for building interactive visualizations and reports. 📂 Data Cleaning & Transformation – Data preparation and structuring (likely with Power Query or similar tools). 🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Creation of calculated KPIs and churn metrics. 📊 Data Modeling – Relationships set up for customer attributes like gender, country, product, and account balance. 🗄️ SQL/Excel – Possible use for initial extraction and validation of banking customer data. 📁 File Format – .pbix for Power BI dashboard design, .jpg/.png for image previews.

4. Data Source: Source: Bank Customer Dataset (Youtube-Kaggle Data Set) The dataset includes 10,000 customer records with details on: Demographic attributes (Gender, Country), Financial features (Credit score, Account balance, Product holding), Behavioral variables (Credit card holding, Activity status), Target variable: Churn status.

5. Features / Highlights: Business Problem: Banks face challenges in retaining customers and minimizing churn, which can impact profitability and customer lifetime value. Goal of the Dashboard: To provide actionable insights on customer churn, enabling business leaders to: Analyze churn rates by customer profiles and behaviors. Detect high-risk segments based on demographics or financial behavior. Prioritize retention efforts where churn risk is highest.

Key Visuals: 📌 KPI: Total Customers , Overall Churn Rate. 📊 Donut Charts: Customer distribution by Gender, Activity Status, Credit Card Ownership, Country, and Product. 📉 Bar & Line Charts: Customers and churn rate by Credit Score. Customers and churn rate by Account Balance. Customers and churn rate by Age Group.

Business Impact & Insights: Gender: Slightly higher female representation (54.6%) than males. Churn: 20.4% of customers are classified as churned, a significant business risk. Product & Country Segments: Majority from Product 1 and from France. Churn Correlation: Highest churn rates among customers aged 51-60 and those with account balances between 10K–100K. Credit Score: Most customers fall in the 701–800 range, but highest churn rates are for those with scores <400. Actionable Areas: High churn among inactive status, product 2 holders, and certain age/credit brackets signal where to focus interventions.
